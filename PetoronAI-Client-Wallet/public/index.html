PetoronAI Client Wallet

Local client-side deposit tool for PetoronAI commercial license payment.

1. Connect RPC and Wallet

Paste your Infura, Alchemy, or other Ethereum Mainnet RPC URL.
This is used locally only while the app is open. Do not share it with anyone.

2. Wallet Status

Check your wallet balance, Vault address, and current Ethereum block.







3. Deposit License Payment




Paste the commitment provided for your PetoronAI license. It must be 0x + 64 hex characters.





Enter the exact ETH amount requested for your license payment.




Before sending, verify the RPC URL, commitment, amount, and wallet balance.









4. Check Commitment


After deposit, paste the same commitment here and verify that it exists in the Vault.
