Companies seeking greater privacy can purchase PetoronAI commercial licenses using Monero.

The procedure is the same as with ETH, but the license fee is converted to XMR.

If desired, you can contact us via an anonymous remailer, disposable email, your corporate email, or any other email address.

So:
1. Follow the visual instructions to create a license.
2. Send a request for a Monero payment address to the email specified in the NOTICE.md file.
3. Receive the address and complete the transaction (after converting the license fee to Monero at the current exchange rate).
4. Contact us by email and attach your commitment and the exact date the transaction was sent in Monero.
5. Receive confirmation of successful license payment.

Then, from the official Petroron address, your commitment will be recorded in smart contract:
0x6BD1A8237b1620D11acB4520b3B2EeF13d7e6516.

This payment procedure is only an additional option and is not mandatory.

The primary payment method remains ETH.
